What It Does

Discover nature, anywhere.

Field-ready identification, a personal sightings log, and a garden planner — all running locally on your machine.

🔍

Species Identifier

Upload any trail photo. LLaVA — an open-weight vision model running locally — delivers a full field guide profile: common name, scientific name, edibility, habitat, and safety notes.

📡

True Offline Mode

Zero signal? No problem. The AI runs entirely on your laptop's CPU or GPU. Five miles deep in the backcountry with no bars — WildGuide still works.

🗺️

Sightings Map

Log GPS-tagged sightings and visualize them on an interactive Folium map. Export your nature journal as JSON — it's yours forever, stored only on your device.

🌱

Garden Planner

Select your USDA hardiness zone and get a week-by-week AI-generated planting calendar tuned to your exact local frost dates. What to plant this week, not last month.

🔒

Total Privacy

Your photos never leave your device. No API key. No cloud upload. No data harvested by a corporation. Your rare species sightings are yours and yours alone.

Why Open-Source AI

Technology that improves time in nature.

WildGuide is built entirely on open-weight AI running locally via Ollama. That openness is the whole point: a hiker 5 miles deep identifying a mushroom before dinner needs this to work without signal. Closed APIs fundamentally cannot do that.

When you photograph rare species or forage for food, your data is sensitive. It shouldn't be uploaded to a server you don't control or harvested for a company's training data. Open-source AI keeps it exactly where it belongs — on your device.

When people understand nature better, they help protect it.
Open vs Closed

Practical technology. Open by design.

❌ Closed API Approach

  • Photos sent to corporate servers
  • Requires internet connection
  • Pay per query — costs add up fast
  • Locked to one provider's model
  • No fine-tuning on local species
  • Your sighting data = their training data

✅ WildGuide (Open Source)

  • Photos never leave your device
  • Works 100% offline on the trail
  • Free forever after one model pull
  • Swap models with a single click
  • Fine-tune on your regional species
  • Your data, your journal, your privacy
